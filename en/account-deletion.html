Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your Visibel account and data in two ways.

In the App (immediate)

Go to Profile > Settings > Privacy > Delete account and confirm with your password. Deletion happens immediately; if it is interrupted you can retry with the same step.

If you cannot access the App (by e-mail)

Send an e-mail with the subject "Account deletion request" from the e-mail address registered to your account to TODO_DOLDUR. After verifying your identity we delete your account within 30 days at the latest.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Account, profile, preferencesDeleted
Test results and answersDeleted; aggregate question statistics with no link to your identity remain
Writing texts, voice recordings, transcriptsNever stored
Vocabulary bank, notes, decksDeleted
Pen pal matches and lettersDeleted; your letters are also removed from the other person's screen
Community answers and likesDeleted
Report recordsThe reported content and your identity are deleted; the existence of the report and the decision may remain without identity
Certificates and share linksDeleted; verification codes stop working
Security and access logsKept for the legally required period, then deleted

If you want only some of your data deleted without deleting your account, write to the same address. You can get a copy of your data before deleting it under Settings > Privacy > Download my data.
