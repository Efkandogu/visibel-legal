5. Retention period

Your data is kept while your account is open. When you delete your account in the App, your data is deleted immediately; deletion requests received by e-mail are completed within 30 days at the latest after identity verification. In report records, the reported content and your identity are deleted; the existence of the report and the decision may remain without identity. Records that must be kept by law are kept for the legally required period and then destroyed.

6. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

By applying to the data controller you have the right to:

  1. learn whether your personal data is processed,
  2. request information if it has been processed,
  3. learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose,
  4. know the third parties to whom it is transferred in Türkiye or abroad,
  5. request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  6. request erasure or destruction under the conditions of Article 7 of the KVKK,
  7. request that the operations in items 5 and 6 be notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred,
  8. object to a result against you arising exclusively from analysis by automated systems,
  9. claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Level results are produced by AI-assisted automated evaluation; they are not official exam results and your right to object is reserved. You can get a copy of your data in the App under Settings > Privacy > Download my data.

7. How to apply

You can send your application with the Application Form: in writing to TODO_DOLDUR, with a secure electronic or mobile signature, or from the e-mail address registered in the App to TODO_DOLDUR.

Applications are concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest; if the process requires an additional cost, the fee in the Board's tariff may be charged.
