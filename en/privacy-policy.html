Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective date: TODO_DOLDUR

Visibel is a mobile application that estimates your foreign-language level (CEFR A1–C2) in reading, listening, writing and speaking, and offers vocabulary practice, a weekly community question and pen pals. The data controller is TODO_DOLDUR, a natural person; contact: TODO_DOLDUR. For details see the Privacy Notice.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: e-mail address, password (encrypted hash), display name.
  • Profile and preferences: native language, language you are learning, interface language, year of birth, skill levels.
  • Test data: whether your answers were right or wrong, your results, the questions shown to you.
  • Writing and speaking: your text, your voice recording and its transcript are processed only for scoring and are not stored; only the level you reach is stored.
  • Vocabulary bank: your words, notes, study history.
  • Pen pal: match information, your letters, your acceptance of the conduct rules, report and block records.
  • Community: your answers to the weekly question, your likes, your reports.
  • AI content reports: the identifier of the content you reported and the reason.
  • Technical data: IP address, session and access logs, usage quota counters.

We do not collect your location, contacts or photos; we do not use your advertising ID, we use no analytics or advertising tools, and we do not sell your data. The microphone works only when you press the record button in the speaking task; the recording is also deleted from your device after scoring. Notifications are created on your device; your notification preferences are kept on your device.

2. How we use data

  • To create your account and provide the service
  • To measure your level, score your writing and speaking and produce a result report
  • To avoid showing you the same question again and to monitor question quality with aggregate statistics
  • To schedule vocabulary reviews and show the reminders you choose
  • To provide pen pal matching and messaging and to prevent abuse
  • To keep the service secure, fix errors and comply with legal obligations

3. Use of artificial intelligence

Questions, text-to-speech audio and writing/speaking scores are produced with Google's Gemini AI service. For scoring, your text or voice recording is sent to Google; your pen pal letters and community answers are checked for the conduct rules with the same service before they are sent. Your name, e-mail address, year of birth and user ID are not sent; your native language may be sent only as the code of the language an explanation should be written in. Under its paid service terms Google does not use this content to improve its models; it may keep it for a limited time to monitor abuse.

Results are estimates produced by AI-assisted automated evaluation; they do not replace an official exam result or certificate. You can report AI content you find wrong or inappropriate with the "Report" option in the App.

4. Who we share with

  • Supabase Inc.: database, authentication, file storage (London, United Kingdom (AWS eu-west-2)).
  • Google LLC (Gemini API): question generation, text-to-speech, transcription, scoring, content checks (United States).
  • Other users: your pen pal sees only the letters you send them and the language/level of the match; your name, e-mail and age are not shown. Your community answers are shown without your name.
  • Authorities: when required by law.

Transfers abroad are made with standard contractual clauses under Article 9 of the KVKK (Turkish Personal Data Protection Law).

5. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is open. You can delete your account immediately in the App (Settings > Privacy > Delete account) or request deletion as described on the Account Deletion page; e-mail requests are completed within 30 days at the latest. Questions in the question pool are not personal data and remain; the link between them and your answers is deleted.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). In the database each user can access only their own data. The access keys of the AI service are kept on the server, not on the device.

7. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13; your year of birth is asked at sign-up and no account is opened below this age. The pen pal feature is available only to users aged 18 or over. In the community question, answers of users under and over 18 are not shown to each other. If we learn that we have collected data from a user below this age, we delete the account.

8. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to access your data, request its correction or deletion, learn to whom it was transferred and object to the result of an automated evaluation. Users in the European Economic Area may also exercise their rights under the GDPR, including data portability. You can get a copy of your data as JSON under Settings > Privacy > Download my data. Contact: TODO_DOLDUR or the Application Form.

9. Changes

When we update this policy we change its version and effective date and announce important changes in the App.
